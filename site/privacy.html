1. What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

In short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, when you participate in activities on the Services (such as creating or joining a room), or otherwise when you contact us.

Personal information provided by you. The personal information we collect may include:

  • email addresses
  • passwords (handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see them)
  • contact or authentication data
  • search preferences: if you have an account, the search settings you choose (distance unit, search radius, number of options, cuisines to prefer or leave out, and whether to show places open now first), so they follow you between devices. They never include your location.
  • your own list: if you have an account, the places you type into We'll Decide What We Want (a name, and optionally an address and a note, for up to 50 places), so your list follows you between devices. Without an account, your list stays only on your device. We never look these places up or connect them to your location.

Sensitive information. When necessary, with your consent or as otherwise permitted by applicable law, we process the following categories of sensitive information: account login information and precise geolocation.

Google sign-in data. You can choose to register or sign in with your Google account. If you do, we receive certain profile information from Google, as described in How do we handle your social logins? below.

Application data. If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to give us access or permission:

  • Geolocation information. When you start a search as a host, we ask for your device's location to find restaurants near you. We ask only at that moment, not continuously or in the background. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings.
  • ZIP code. Instead of your device's location, you can choose to search around a US ZIP code. We use it only to find the middle of that ZIP code's area for your search. This ZIP code is not saved and will be forgotten after the app is closed.

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.

Information automatically collected

In short: Some information, such as your Internet Protocol (IP) address and browser and device characteristics, is collected automatically when you use our Services.

We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use, or navigate the Services. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information). It is needed to keep our Services secure and working, and we do not use it for analytics or advertising. The information we collect includes:

  • Guest identifier. When you use the app without an account, we create a random guest identifier so you can join rooms and vote.
  • Log and usage data. Our servers automatically record service-related and diagnostic information when you use our Services, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the date and time of each request, and whether it succeeded.
  • Security checks. To keep automated abuse out, our bot-protection provider checks that requests come from a genuine browser or app. This reads browser and device signals and may set its own cookie.
  • Location data. When you start a search as a host, we use your device's location, rounded to about 100 meters, to find nearby restaurants. You can refuse access to your location or disable it in your device settings; however, if you do, you will not be able to start a search near you. You can search around a ZIP code instead, and you can still join rooms and vote.

You can find out more about cookies and similar technologies in our Cookie Policy: https://getwhatdoyouwant.app/cookies.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

12. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. We do not track you across other websites in any case. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
