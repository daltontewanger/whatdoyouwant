Privacy policy

Our full privacy policy is being finalized before launch. In short: we ask for your location only when you start a search, use it once to find nearby places, and never store it. Accounts keep only what they need to run rooms and your saved lists, and you can delete your account at any time from the app.

Questions before then? See Support. To delete your account and its data, see Delete your account.
