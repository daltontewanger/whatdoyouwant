Cookie policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how What Do You Want?! ("we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website at https://getwhatdoyouwant.app and use our web app ("Website"). It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

In short: we use only what is needed to keep you signed in, bring you back to your room, and protect the Website from automated abuse. We do not use cookies or similar technologies for analytics, advertising, or tracking you across other websites, so we do not show a cookie banner.

In some cases these technologies are linked to personal information, such as the account you are signed in to. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently. Websites can also keep similar data in your browser's local storage or database storage; we describe those here too.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, What Do You Want?!) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies." Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website. The only third-party cookie on our Website is set by Google reCAPTCHA, our bot-protection service.

Why do we use cookies?

Everything we use is "essential" or "strictly necessary": the Website cannot work properly or securely without it. We do not use cookies to track or target your interests, and no third party serves cookies through our Website for advertising or analytics.

How can I control cookies?

Because everything listed above is essential, there is no setting to switch it off within the Website. You can set or amend your web browser controls to block or delete cookies and site data. If you do, you will be signed out, the Website will not remember your room, and the security check may stop you from using the app until you allow it again.

How can I control cookies on my browser?

As the means by which you can refuse cookies through your web browser controls vary from browser to browser, you should visit your browser's help menu for more information. The following is information about how to manage cookies on the most popular browsers:

What about other tracking technologies, like web beacons?

We do not use web beacons, tracking pixels, or similar technologies on our Website or in emails.

Do you serve targeted advertising?

No. We do not show advertising, and no advertising companies place cookies through our Website. If that changes, we will update this Cookie Policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it before any advertising cookies are used.

How often will you update this Cookie Policy?

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please therefore revisit this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.

The date at the top of this Cookie Policy indicates when it was last updated.
