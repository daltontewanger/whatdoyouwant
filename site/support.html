Support

Need a hand? Email us at what.do.you.want.theapp@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to tell us what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and whether you were on a phone, tablet, or computer.

Common questions

How do I join a room?

Ask the host for the room code, link, or QR code. Open the link or scan the code with your phone's camera, or open the app and type the code. You can join as a guest without an account.

Why can't I start a search?

Starting a room's search needs an account with a verified email address. Each account also has a weekly search limit that keeps the app free, and searches can be paused for everyone for a while. Guests can always join rooms and vote.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only the host's location is used, only when they start a search, to find restaurants nearby. It is rounded to about 100 meters and not stored. You can turn location access off in your device or browser settings; you can still join rooms and vote.

A restaurant's details are wrong or it has closed

Restaurant details come from a third-party data provider, so they can be out of date. Please check with the restaurant before you go, and let us know what was wrong so we can pass it on.

Your privacy requests

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: open Your account and choose Delete account. This removes your account and its data right away. See Delete your account for details, including what to do if you cannot sign in.
  • Get a copy of your data or correct it by emailing us from the address on your account. We will reply within the time the law requires, which is 30 to 45 days depending on where you live.
  • Guest data is deleted automatically after 30 days of inactivity.

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and your rights in full.

Report a problem or abuse

If someone is misusing a room or the app, or something is not working, email what.do.you.want.theapp@gmail.com. If you can, include the room code and roughly when it happened.
