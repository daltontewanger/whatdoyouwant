Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time, and it takes effect right away.

In the app

  1. Open Your account in the app.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your memberships in rooms and your votes.
  • Rooms you are hosting are closed for everyone.

Server logs that may include your IP address are deleted within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

If you cannot sign in

Email what.do.you.want.theapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm the request so we know it came from you, and we will delete the account and let you know, within 30 days.

If you used the app as a guest

There is no account to delete. Guest data is deleted automatically after 30 days of inactivity, and you can remove it from your device sooner by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
