Why you're going to want it

Open any tile to learn more.

15

Everyone gets a say

Up to 15 people swipe the same picks, so no one decides alone.

A room holds up to 15 people. Everyone gets the same set of nearby spots and votes on their own phone, at their own pace. When everyone is done, the group sees the spot most people said yes to, plus a backup in case it's closed or full.

Fresh picks every time

A new random draw from your whole distance.

Every room draws a new random set of spots from anywhere inside the distance you choose, not just the closest handful. Start another room and you get a different mix, so you find new places instead of the same ones every time.

I know what I want

Save favorites, rule out nevers.

While you vote, mark a spot as a favorite: it counts as a yes and is saved to your account as one of your "I know what I want" spots. Turn favorites on and they can show up in your rooms more often. Vote never instead and it counts as a no, and that spot stays out of your future rooms. Saving favorites and nevers needs an account, so we can keep them for you.

No download to join

Friends join from their browser with a code, a link or a QR code.

Friends scan the QR code, open the link or type the code, and join right in their phone's browser as a guest. No app and no account needed to join and vote. Only the host needs a verified account to start a room.
