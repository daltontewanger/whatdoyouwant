Accessibility

Last updated November 11, 2026

We want everyone to be able to decide where to eat with their friends. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this website and the app.

What we do

  • Every page and control on this website works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline.
  • Images, icons, and controls have text labels for screen readers, and decorative graphics are hidden from them.
  • Text and buttons meet AA color contrast.
  • You can zoom or enlarge text without losing content.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the website's animations stop.
  • The website still reads in full if scripts are turned off.
  • The app supports screen readers and follows your device's text size settings, in the mobile app and in a web browser.
  • In the app, you can vote by tapping the thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons instead of swiping cards. Screen readers announce them as "Vote Yes" and "Vote No".
  • The app's Accessibility features page (in Account) lets you:
    • make the app's text larger, on top of your device's setting;
    • turn off the vote timer, so you can vote at your own pace instead of 15 seconds a card. It turns on by itself while a screen reader or switch control is in use, and it applies to the whole room;
    • turn on screen reader support in a web browser, remembered on that device.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the app's moving pictures, such as the Home screen banner and the loading animation, hold still.

Known limitations

  • In a web browser, the app's screen reader support is off until you turn it on, either with the "Enable accessibility" button that screen readers announce when the app opens or with the Screen reader support switch on the Accessibility features page. Keeping it on all the time would make the app slower for everyone else.

Feedback

If something gets in your way, please tell us at what.do.you.want.theapp@gmail.com, or through Support. Let us know the page or screen, what you were trying to do, and the device and any assistive technology you use. We aim to reply within 5 business days and will work to fix the problem.
