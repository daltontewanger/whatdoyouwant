Accessibility

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA across this site and the app: keyboard access, screen reader labels, readable contrast, text that can be enlarged, and less motion when your device asks for it. Swiping always has button alternatives.

If something gets in your way, tell us through Support and we will fix it.
